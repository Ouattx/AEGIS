# Target operators: first outreach list

Built from public information on 25 October 2026. **Verify each company's current status,
ownership and contacts before reaching out**: the telecom market consolidates often (for
example, Alsatis's telecom business was taken over by Nordnet in October 2023, and Adista
now operates as inherent).

Who to contact at each operator: the **CISO / RSSI**, the **head of network security or NOC**,
and the **abuse desk manager**. Find them on LinkedIn. Do not send sales messages to
public `abuse@` or NOC mailboxes: those are for incident reports, and misusing them damages
your reputation with exactly the people you want to work with.

## Tier 3: business-only ISPs (fewer TV boxes; later)

Netalis, inherent (formerly Adista), SFR Business, Bouygues Telecom Entreprises, Orange Business.
Their customers are companies, so the TV-box story fits less well. The egress-abuse and evidence
features still apply.

## Not direct targets

Wholesale fiber infrastructure operators (for example XpFibre, Axione, Altitude Infra) mostly
build and lease networks and do not run subscribers' internet traffic, so they are not SHIELD
customers. They can be useful introducers to the retail ISPs on their networks.
